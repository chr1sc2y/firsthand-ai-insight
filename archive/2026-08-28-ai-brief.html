Google DeepMind Pilots Double-Blind AI Evaluations

HIGH

Facts

  • Google DeepMind is piloting the world's first double-blind evaluation of a proprietary frontier AI model, using confidential computing to keep test prompts and model weights private. S002 S030
  • The pilot involves partners including the Singapore AI Safety Institute, OpenMined, AVERI, and MLCommons. S002
  • The approach uses Google Cloud's Confidential Space to cryptographically verify that neither party can see the other's data. S002

Analysis. This initiative addresses the critical issue of benchmark contamination, potentially setting a new standard for trustworthy AI evaluation.

Implication. Enterprises and policymakers can have greater confidence in AI benchmark results, leading to more informed decisions.

Uncertainty. The effectiveness of the cryptographic environment in real-world scenarios and its adoption by other AI labs are not yet clear.
